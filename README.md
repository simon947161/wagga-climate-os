# Wagga ClimateOS

Wagga ClimateOS is a regional prototype for applying climate intelligence and evidence-based planning to Wagga Wagga and the Riverina.

This repository explores how a regional ClimateOS could help organise local climate risks, development pressures, infrastructure needs, energy opportunities and sustainability pathways. The project is grounded in the belief that climate adaptation must become practical, local and usable.

## Regional focus

The Riverina faces a mix of challenges and opportunities:

- Heat and drought risk
- Water security
- Regional housing and infrastructure growth
- Agricultural transition
- Renewable energy potential
- Community resilience
- Local government planning pressure
- ESG and sustainability reporting needs

Wagga ClimateOS aims to turn these scattered issues into a structured regional intelligence framework.

## Long-term vision

The long-term goal is to create a practical model that can be reused by other regional towns and communities.

A ClimateOS should not only belong to global institutions or large cities. Small towns and regional communities also need their own evidence systems, future planning tools and resilience intelligence.
